# Observed Competitive Brawl Meta

This note aggregates opponents encountered across tracked decks. It should not be treated as a statistically representative Arena metagame until the sample becomes much larger.

## Recurring themes from the Squall sample — 60 games

### Commander-centric engines
Many opponents rely on resolving or repeatedly deploying their commander. Drannith Magistrate, Disruptor Flute and edict effects have repeatedly performed well. Recent examples include Ketramose, Narset, Sephiroth, Gut, Oura, Tymna/Thrasios and Captain America shells.

### Artifact / enchantment engines
A substantial number of difficult games have involved noncreature permanents generating repeated value: Paradox Engine, Forsaken Monument, equipment engines, Omniscience, Bitterblossom-style engines and artifact shells. Broad permanent interaction remains more valuable than creature-only removal in the observed field.

### Graveyard pressure / hate
Graveyard recursion is common, but so is graveyard denial. G57 vs Oura showed Valgavoth shutting off Squall's graveyard access, forcing a pivot toward equipment/evasion/lifelink.

### Free/cheap permission
Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Daze, Flare of Denial, Mana Drain and Mana Leak have repeatedly disrupted setup. G59 again showed Force of Negation in a partner-control shell.

### Equipment / Boros engines
Sram/Captain America-style equipment decks reinforce the importance of killing setup engines before equipment chains snowball. G60 showed Get Lost answering Sram early while Skrelv protected Squall through red removal.

### Explosive green ramp / landfall
Tifa, Mythweaver Poq, Lumra, Primeval Titan/Grist and Golos-style lines demonstrate that green decks can move from parity to overwhelming board/mana states quickly.

### Fast aggro
Merry, Hawkeye, Iron Man and Gut show that early board pressure remains common enough to justify cheap interaction and sweepers.

### 5C value/control
Hei Bai, Omnath, Atraxa and Esika variants can chain multiple engines and premium interaction. Early disruption remains important before their card-advantage layer becomes overwhelming.

## Cards/engines repeatedly relevant on the opposing side

- Strip Mine / land denial
- Free or highly efficient counterspells
- Graveyard recursion and graveyard shutdown
- Primeval Titan / large ramp payoffs
- Artifact and enchantment value engines
- Commander protection effects
- Equipment engines such as Sram/Boots shells
- Sweepers that reset Squall's board while enabling the opponent to rebuild
