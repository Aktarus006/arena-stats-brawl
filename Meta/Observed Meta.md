# Observed Competitive Brawl Meta

This note aggregates opponents encountered across tracked decks. It should not be treated as a statistically representative Arena metagame until the sample becomes much larger.

## Recurring themes from the Squall sample — 56 games

### Commander-centric engines
Many opponents rely on resolving or repeatedly deploying their commander. Drannith Magistrate, Disruptor Flute and edict effects have repeatedly performed well. Recent examples include Ketramose, Narset, Sephiroth and Gut.

### Artifact / enchantment engines
A substantial number of difficult games have involved noncreature permanents generating repeated value: Paradox Engine, Forsaken Monument, equipment engines, Omniscience, Bitterblossom-style engines and artifact shells. Broad permanent interaction is therefore more valuable than creature-only removal in the observed field.

### Graveyard / land recursion
Seen through Slimefoot and Squee, Leovold, Lumra, Grist and Ketramose. Recurring fetchlands, Strip Mine, creatures and toolbox pieces remains a significant axis of play.

### Explosive green ramp / landfall
Tifa, Mythweaver Poq, Lumra, Primeval Titan/Grist and Golos-style lines demonstrate that green decks can move from parity to overwhelming board/mana states very quickly.

### Fast aggro
Merry, Hawkeye, Iron Man and Gut show that early board pressure remains common enough to justify cheap interaction and sweepers. Cori-Steel Cutter was particularly impactful in G56.

### 5C value/control
Hei Bai, Omnath and Atraxa variants can chain multiple engines and premium interaction. Early disruption is important before their card-advantage layer becomes overwhelming.

### Free/cheap permission
Force of Will, Force of Negation, Fierce Guardianship, Daze, Flare of Denial, Mana Drain and Mana Leak have repeatedly disrupted Squall's attempts to stabilize or establish its commander. Protected-turn effects and discard remain important alongside removal.

## Cards/engines repeatedly relevant on the opposing side

- Strip Mine / land denial
- Free or highly efficient counterspells
- Graveyard recursion engines
- Primeval Titan / large ramp payoffs
- Artifact and enchantment value engines
- Commander protection effects such as Giver of Runes
- Sweepers that reset Squall's board while enabling the opponent to rebuild

Update this page as more decks and games are logged.
