# Observed Competitive Brawl Meta

This note aggregates opponents encountered across tracked decks. It should not be treated as a statistically representative Arena metagame until the sample becomes much larger.

## Recurring themes from the Squall sample

### Graveyard / land recursion
Seen through Slimefoot and Squee, Leovold, Lumra and Grist. Recurring fetchlands, Strip Mine, creatures and toolbox pieces is a significant axis of play.

### Explosive green ramp / landfall
Tifa, Mythweaver Poq, Lumra and Grist/Primeval Titan lines demonstrate that green decks can move from parity to overwhelming board/mana states very quickly.

### Commander-centric engines
Drannith Magistrate and Disruptor Flute repeatedly performed well because many opposing decks rely heavily on resolving their commander.

### 5C value/control
Hei Bai and Omnath variants can chain multiple engines and premium interaction. Early disruption is important before their card-advantage layer becomes overwhelming.

### Aggro / legends
Merry and Hawkeye demonstrate the need to respect early pressure and carefully manage self-inflicted damage.

## Cards/engines repeatedly observed on the opposing side

- Toxic Deluge
- Strip Mine recursion
- Primeval Titan
- Field of the Dead
- Endurance
- Green Sun's Zenith
- Mana Drain / Force of Will style permission
- Graveyard recursion engines

Update this page as more decks and games are logged.
