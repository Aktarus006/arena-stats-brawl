# Squall — Matchups

## Tifa / explosive landfall

**Observed:** 1–2.

- Loss #1 involved a likely pilot error around Ripples/Dismember.
- Win came from removing Tifa early with Werefox and stabilizing with Sheoldred.
- Loss #2 involved Traveling Chocobo doubling triggers and snowballing too quickly.

**Working plan:** mulligan more aggressively for cheap interaction. Prioritize killing the engine before it receives multiple landfall/doubling triggers.

## Merry / Boros legends aggro

**Observed:** 1–1.

- First match: overwhelmed by a wide, protected legends board.
- Rematch: killed Merry twice early (Lotho block, flashed Spider-Man) and opponent conceded.

**Working plan:** commander removal is a priority; denying Merry's card-flow engine is more efficient than trying to answer every secondary legend.

## Graveyard / land recursion

Notable opponents: Slimefoot and Squee, Leovold, Lumra, Grist.

**Successful tools:** Kutzil's Flanker, Boggart Trawler, Dauthi Voidwalker, Strip Mine recursion, Disruptor Flute.

**Risk:** highly redundant engines can answer the first graveyard-hate effect and continue via tutors/lands (e.g. Endurance into GSZ/Primeval Titan/Field of the Dead).

**Watch:** whether another cheap repeatable/recursive grave-hate permanent is justified.

## 5C value/control

Notable opponents: Hei Bai variants, Phyrexian Omnath.

These decks can overwhelm Squall if they establish a multi-layer engine (Sanctum of All, Atraxa, Yarok/Elesh Norn-style value). Early discard, Drannith, Flute and Opposition Agent-style effects are especially important.

## Commander-centric expensive decks

Etali was a clean example: Drannith Magistrate plus Recruiter → Skyclave dismantled both commander access and acceleration.

**Working plan:** prioritize hatebear/commander tax effects, then attack mana rocks or enabling permanents.

## Fast red/aggro

Hawkeye game was close; Meathook materially extended the game but opponent topdecked lethal.

**Working concern:** track how much damage is opponent-generated versus self-inflicted by Ancient Tomb, Mana Confluence, Bob, Ripples, Thoughtseize and fetch/shock sequencing.

## Board wipes

Results are mixed rather than structurally bad:

- Amalia: Toxic Deluge caused a major loss of board and opponent rebuilt faster.
- Leovold rematch: won through Toxic Deluge.
- Pantlaza: won through two board wipes once Squall began recurring resources.

**Working plan:** avoid unnecessary overextension; Squall/Lurrus can make the graveyard a superior reconstruction engine after wipes.
