# Squall V2.1 — Games 51–current

**Status:** active testing

**Start:** Game 51

**Current record:** 5–4 after Game 59 (55.6%)

## Changes from V2

### IN
- Loran of the Third Path
- Sheltered by Ghosts
- Plaguecrafter
- Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer

### OUT
- Braids, Arisen Nightmare
- The Sackville-Bagginses
- Kaya's Ghostform
- Necropotence

## Goal
Shift further toward an **Orzhov disruptive recursive toolbox**: interaction that is independently strong, especially creature-based ETB interaction that Recruiter/Squall/Lurrus/Ral can reuse, while retaining alternate non-graveyard paths to victory.

## Early observations — Games 51–59

- **Plaguecrafter:** strong first showing in G52 alongside Lurrus and Ral; sacrifice interaction helped answer Ketramose repeatedly.
- **Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer:** strong first showing in G52; G59 was an even stronger validation, returning Ranger-Captain for another tutor and later Accursed Marauder to answer a reanimated Tymna.
- **Sheltered by Ghosts:** awkward first observation in creature-light Narset G53, then a **major positive in G57**, where its lifelink was crucial to winning a long race after Valgavoth disabled graveyard recursion.
- **Erode:** useful in G56, removing Tersa and enabling a Reanimate line.
- **Sheoldred's Edict:** strong in G53 against Narset and again in G59, answering Tymna before Ral/Marauder handled her second appearance.
- **Accursed Marauder:** matchup floor was visible in G53, but G59 showed the ceiling when recurred by Ral to remove a key non-token commander.
- **Ranger-Captain of Eos:** G59 showed strong recursive toolbox value: first found Mother of Runes, then after Ral returned it post-Toxic Deluge it found Esper Sentinel.
- **Drannith Magistrate:** strong again in G57, keeping Oura out of normal commander deployment.
- **Cloud → Buster Sword + Key to the City** gave the deck a successful alternate combat plan in G57 when recursion was unavailable.
- **G58 vs Esika:** Gemstone Caverns enabled T1 Voice while on the draw; T2 equipment into T3 Dark Ritual → Squall plus Static Prison on a mana rock forced an early concession.
- **G59 vs Tymna/Thrasios:** the deck won through Force of Negation, Toxic Deluge, Dismember and opposing Reanimate. Ral converted graveyard creatures into exact answers/value, demonstrating the intended V2.1 engine at a very high level.

V2.1's first nine games are **5–4 (55.6%)**. The wins now span multiple axes: independent recursion without Squall (G52), discard → Reanimate theft (G55), combat through graveyard lock (G57), explosive disruptive tempo (G58), and recursive toolbox grinding through premium interaction (G59). This is the strongest structural evidence so far that V2.1 is healthier than V2, though the sample remains small.
