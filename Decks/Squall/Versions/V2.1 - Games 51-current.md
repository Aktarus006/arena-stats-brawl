## Goal
Shift further toward an **Orzhov disruptive recursive toolbox**: interaction that is independently strong, especially creature-based ETB interaction that Recruiter/Squall/Lurrus/Ral can reuse.

## Early observations — Games 51–56

- **Plaguecrafter:** strong first showing in G52 alongside Lurrus and Ral; sacrifice interaction helped answer Ketramose repeatedly.
- **Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer:** strong first showing in G52; contributed meaningful recursion/value in a game won without casting Squall.
- **Sheltered by Ghosts:** seen in G53 but matchup against low-creature Narset reduced its immediate utility.
- **Erode:** useful in G56, removing Tersa and enabling a Reanimate line.
- **Sheoldred's Edict:** strong in G53 against Narset; sacrifice effects continue to justify their place against protected commanders.
- V2.1's first six games are **2–4**, but several losses were close or involved matchup/resource-specific issues rather than interaction simply failing to exist.
