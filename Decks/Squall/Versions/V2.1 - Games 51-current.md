# Squall V2.1 — Games 51–current

**Status:** active testing

**Start:** Game 51

**Current record:** 6–4 after Game 60 (60.0%)

## Changes from V2

### IN
- Loran of the Third Path
- Sheltered by Ghosts
- Plaguecrafter
- Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer

### OUT
- Braids, Arisen Nightmare
- The Sackville-Bagginses
- Kaya's Ghostform
- Necropotence

## Goal
Shift further toward an **Orzhov disruptive recursive toolbox**: interaction that is independently strong, especially creature-based ETB interaction that Recruiter/Squall/Lurrus/Ral can reuse, while retaining alternate non-graveyard paths to victory.

## First 10-game block — Games 51–60

**Record: 6–4 (60.0%)**.

- G52: Plaguecrafter + Lurrus + Ral won a long game without casting Squall.
- G55: discard plus Reanimate converted opposing Trophy Mage into proactive value.
- G57: graveyard recursion was shut off by Valgavoth, but Sheltered by Ghosts lifelink + Cloud/Buster Sword + Key won through combat.
- G58: Gemstone Caverns + Voice + equipment + Dark Ritual/Squall + Static Prison produced an explosive tempo concession.
- G59: Ral repeatedly returned exact toolbox creatures after Toxic Deluge, with Ranger-Captain and Accursed Marauder dismantling Tymna/Thrasios.
- G60: Skrelv protected Squall through Lightning Bolt, Get Lost removed Sram, Chrome Mox accelerated Squall, then Squall's double recursion returned both Skrelv and a fetchland to produce protection plus resource growth.

## Card signals

- **Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer:** strongly positive after G52 and especially G59; now a validated secondary recursion/toolbox engine.
- **Sheltered by Ghosts:** strongly positive after G57; interaction/protection/lifelink gives it multiple useful modes.
- **Plaguecrafter / Accursed Marauder / Sheoldred's Edict:** diversified sacrifice interaction has proven valuable against commander-centric decks.
- **Skrelv:** G60 reinforces its role as both protection and a premium recursion target.
- **Get Lost:** G60 cleanly answered an opposing Sram engine before it snowballed.

## Checkpoint conclusion

V2.1's first complete 10-game block finished **6–4**, compared with V2's **1–9**. That is not enough to declare the rebuild solved, but it is the strongest evidence so far that the post-audit direction is healthier. The wins also arrived through multiple distinct plans rather than one repeated high-roll, which supports the structural change toward a disruptive toolbox with several independent engines.
