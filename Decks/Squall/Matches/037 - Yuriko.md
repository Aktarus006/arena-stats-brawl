The final sequence again demonstrates how difficult it is to force through a decisive spell against Yuriko while the opponent retains free/low-cost interaction and flash creatures.

## Matchup signal
Second recorded loss to Yuriko (Games 31 and 37). Game 31 was dominated by concentrated free counters; Game 37 went very long and nearly ended in a Squall win. Continue tracking **Grand Abolisher / Voice of Victory / Ranger-Captain / discard** as ways to create a protected lethal window rather than simply adding more removal.
