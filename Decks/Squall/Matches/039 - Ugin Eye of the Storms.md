# Game 39 — Ugin, Eye of the Storms

## Result
Loss.

## Key sequence
The opponent's **Forsaken Monument** generated enough repeated life gain that racing became unsustainable.

Squall successfully exiled **two Ulamog threats**, showing that the deck could answer individual giant creatures. However, once the opponent resolved the **Ugin planeswalker**, the game became effectively unwinnable.

## Diagnosis
This was less a failure of creature removal than a failure to disrupt the opponent's permanent-based mana/value engine. Answering two Ulamogs consumed interaction but did not address **Forsaken Monument**, whose life gain removed the possibility of winning through normal combat pressure while the colorless deck continued to scale into larger threats.

This reinforces the signal from Game 38: some matchups require interaction with **artifacts/enchantments/planeswalkers or engines themselves**, rather than additional creature removal.

## Deckbuilding signal
Second consecutive game where a durable noncreature permanent engine was central to the loss. Continue tracking whether **Cathar Commando** is sufficient as the recursive artifact/enchantment answer and whether the toolbox needs another broad permanent answer. Prefer flexible interaction over narrow artifact hate if a change becomes justified.
