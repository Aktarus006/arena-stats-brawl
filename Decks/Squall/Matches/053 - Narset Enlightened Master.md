# Game 53 — Narset, Enlightened Master

## Result
Loss.

## Opening / early game
The opening hand was highly disruptive:

- **Fatal Push**
- **Sheltered by Ghosts**
- **Sheoldred's Edict**
- **Werefox Bodyguard**

However, the matchup presented very few creature targets, making part of that interaction awkward.

The opponent disrupted development with:
- **Lightning Helix** on Barrowgoyf
- **Mana Leak** on Key to the City
- **Curse of Silence**

## Key interaction
**Sheoldred's Edict** successfully answered Narset.

A later **Vampiric Tutor** attempted to find **Disruptor Flute**, which was one of the best tools available for slowing the commander-centric plan.

## Matchup mismatch
**Accursed Marauder** was poor here because the only creatures applying pressure were Monk tokens, which Marauder could not meaningfully answer in the reported board state.

This is a useful reminder that sacrifice-on-creature bodies are excellent against singular protected threats, but can be inefficient against token pressure or largely creatureless control/combo shells.

## Opponent endgame
The opponent eventually resolved **Omniscience**, which converted into overwhelming planeswalker/value development including **Ugin** and **Teferi**.

## Diagnosis
This was less a lack-of-interaction loss than an **interaction-shape mismatch**. Squall drew plenty of answers, but too many were creature-focused while Narset's real engine operated through noncreature spells and permanents.

Positive signals:
- **Sheoldred's Edict**: strong, clean answer to Narset.
- **Disruptor Flute**: again one of the best available tools against commander-centric decks.
- **Sheltered by Ghosts** was seen, but the matchup offered fewer attractive creature-board contexts than expected.

Negative/conditional signal:
- **Accursed Marauder** is matchup-dependent and weaker against token-based or creature-light strategies.
