# Game 59 — Tymna + Thrasios

## Result
Win after a mulligan to six in a long, interaction-heavy game.

## Key sequence
- Turn 1: **Thoughtseize** was answered by **Force of Negation**.
- Turn 2: **Accursed Marauder** removed **Thrasios**.
- Turn 3: **Ranger-Captain of Eos** found **Mother of Runes**.
- Turn 4: **Dark Ritual** enabled **Squall, SeeD Mercenary** plus Mother of Runes.
- Opponent answered the developed board with **Toxic Deluge**.

Squall had already pushed meaningful damage before the wipe. The rebuild was strong:
- **Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer** entered and used -2 to return **Ranger-Captain of Eos**.
- Ranger found **Esper Sentinel**.
- Opponent cast **Tymna**; **Sheoldred's Edict** answered her while Ral's +1 surveilled 2.
- Opponent later **Reanimated Tymna**.
- After an opposing **Dismember** had removed Barrowgoyf, Ral's -2 returned **Accursed Marauder**, forcing Tymna to be sacrificed again.
- Opponent conceded after Tymna was removed for the second time.

## Diagnosis
This is one of the clearest demonstrations yet of the V2.1 toolbox engine. The deck absorbed free permission, a board wipe, spot removal and opposing reanimation, then rebuilt through **Ral → Ranger → tutor** and **Ral → Marauder → edict** lines.

The important structural point is that Ral was not merely generic card advantage: it converted specific creatures in the graveyard into the exact interaction/value needed at each stage. Ranger-Captain and Accursed Marauder were both materially stronger because they could be reused.

This also strengthens the case for diversified sacrifice interaction. Tymna was answered first by Sheoldred's Edict and then by a recurred Accursed Marauder, making protection/targeting less relevant.
