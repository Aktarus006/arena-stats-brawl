# Game 55 — Hidetsugu and Kairi

## Result
Win by early concession.

## Key sequence
- Turn 1: **Inquisition of Kozilek** took the opponent's **Trophy Mage**.
- Turn 2: **Thalia, Guardian of Thraben** was deployed, taxing the opponent's noncreature development.
- Turn 3: **Reanimate** brought the opponent's Trophy Mage onto Squall's side of the battlefield.
- Opponent conceded immediately afterward.

## MVP
- **Inquisition of Kozilek** — disrupted the opponent's early development and placed a useful creature in the graveyard.
- **Reanimate** — converted the discard spell into proactive board/value advantage by stealing Trophy Mage.

## Diagnosis
A very clean example of the deck's disruptive-tempo identity: early hand disruption removed a relevant piece, Thalia increased the opponent's friction, and Reanimate turned the opponent's lost resource into Squall's own development.

This is also a reminder that Reanimate's ceiling in the deck is not limited to recycling Squall's own creatures. Discard/removal can make the opponent's graveyard into a toolbox as well.
