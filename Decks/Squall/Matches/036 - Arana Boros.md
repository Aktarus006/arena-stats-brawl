# Game 36 — Boros Araña

## Result
Win.

## Development
An opposing **Thalia, Guardian of Thraben** slowed Squall's development. The deck nevertheless managed to establish **Grand Abolisher**, then used **Dark Ritual** to accelerate **Squall, SeeD Mercenary** onto the battlefield.

The opponent no longer had enough resources to keep pace once Squall was established behind the Abolisher effect.

## Diagnosis
Strong example of the deck winning through opposing taxation rather than simply curving uncontested. **Dark Ritual** converted a constrained mana situation into a tempo swing, while **Grand Abolisher** helped create a safe window for Squall to resolve/develop.

This is a positive data point for the anti-interaction/protection package and for Dark Ritual as more than just an explosive T2-Squall card.
