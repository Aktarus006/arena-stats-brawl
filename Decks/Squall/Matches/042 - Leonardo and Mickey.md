# Game 42 — Leonardo, the Balance + Mickey

## Result
Loss.

## Development
The opponent developed faster and assembled a synergistic engine around **Bitterblossom** plus an Abzan Tarkir commander/permanent that rewarded sacrificing creatures by placing +1/+1 counters across the rest of the board.

The recurring token production provided sacrifice material, while the counter engine continuously converted those sacrifices into permanent board-wide growth. Once established, the board scaled faster than Squall could contain it with individual exchanges.

## Diagnosis
This was an **engine/snowball loss** rather than a simple failure to answer one creature. Bitterblossom supplied renewable material and the sacrifice payoff multiplied that material into an increasingly large board.

The matchup illustrates why the V2 needs both early interaction against engine pieces and reset effects. A timely **Farewell / Toxic Deluge / The Meathook Massacre** can attack the resulting board, while flexible interaction is preferable before the engine becomes established.

## V2 tracking
Squall V2 record: **0-2** after Games 41–42. Both losses involved opposing synergy engines that multiplied board development faster than Squall's small-creature/value plan could keep pace. Sample remains far too small for a V2 verdict.
