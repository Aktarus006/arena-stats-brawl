# Game 43 — Iron Man

## Result
Loss.

## Development
The opponent had a very fast Izzet artifact-aggro start and developed faster than Squall could stabilize.

**Cathar Commando** provided relevant interaction against the artifact plan, but a single artifact-removal body was not enough to contain the amount of early pressure and development.

## Diagnosis
This is useful evidence because Cathar Commando was drawn and did the job it is included for, yet the matchup still overwhelmed Squall. The issue therefore appears broader than simply lacking access to artifact removal: against fast artifact decks, the deck may need either multiple cheap exchanges in the opening turns or a sufficiently early reset effect.

This differs from Games 38–39, where durable artifact engines accumulated value over time. Here the primary problem was **tempo and board development speed**.

## V2 tracking
Squall V2 record: **0-3** after Games 41–43. All three losses have involved opposing engines or development that snowballed faster than Squall could stabilize. Continue testing before changing the list, but specifically track whether the new interaction cards are being drawn and whether their mana costs are low enough to matter against these starts.
