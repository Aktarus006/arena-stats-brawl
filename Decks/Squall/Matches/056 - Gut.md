# Game 56 — Gut

## Result
Loss vs mono-red aggro.

## Development
The opening disruptive package did meaningful work, but an opposing **Strip Mine** cut Squall off mana and the deck failed to draw enough replacement lands afterward. **Voice of Victory** and **Drannith Magistrate** both disrupted the opponent but were answered.

**Erode** removed **Tersa Lightshatter**, and Squall attempted to turn the exchange around by **Reanimating** Tersa. With the opponent eventually empty-handed, **Vampiric Tutor** found **The Meathook Massacre**, cast for X=1 to clear five creatures.

Despite that reset, the early pressure from **Gut** and **Cori-Steel Cutter**, plus **Chain Lightning**, had already reduced the available margin enough that the commander pressure closed the game.

## MVP
- **The Meathook Massacre** — five-for-one board reset and the strongest comeback line available.
- **Erode** — relevant V2 interaction; answered Tersa and enabled the later Reanimate line.

## Diagnosis
This loss combines **opponent-driven mana denial** with fast red pressure. The disruptive package was effective and the deck eventually produced a major stabilization turn, but losing a land to Strip Mine followed by missing replacement land draws prevented development at the pace required.

Importantly, this is another positive observation for V2's interaction package: **Erode was drawn and useful**. The failure was not that the deck lacked meaningful interaction; it was that early resource denial plus accumulated aggro damage left too little recovery time.
