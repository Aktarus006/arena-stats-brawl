# Game 52 — Ketramose

## Result
Win in a long, interaction-heavy game.

## Development
Squall drew a high density of interaction and was able to answer **Ketramose four times** over the course of the game.

The opponent's graveyard-hate pieces interfered with Squall's own recursion/value plan, but those pieces were eventually answered as well.

Notably, the game was won **without ever casting Squall from the command zone**. Land development was relatively sparse, but the deck continued functioning through its secondary engines and interaction.

## Key performers
- **Plaguecrafter** performed strongly as a sacrifice-based answer.
- **Lurrus of the Dream-Den** provided recurring value.
- **Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer** contributed meaningful graveyard/recursion value and worked well alongside the interaction package.

## Diagnosis
This is a very important V2.1 data point. The deck did not require Squall to function: interaction contained the opposing commander repeatedly, while Lurrus/Ral/Plaguecrafter formed an independent value engine.

That is exactly the structural goal of the post-50-game rebuild: **play strong interactive cards first, then let Squall enhance them rather than requiring Squall for the deck to operate**.

The fact that Ketramose was answered four separate times also demonstrates the value of diversified/repeatable interaction against commander-centric strategies.
