# Game 32 — Kefka

## Result
Loss.

## Opening / development
**Gemstone Caverns T0** gave an accelerated start, but subsequent land development stalled and the deck could not keep pace.

## Decisive sequence
- T1 **Mother of Runes** → **Unholy Heat**.
- T2 **Grand Abolisher** → **Mana Leak**.
- T3 **Voice of Victory** → **Mana Drain**.
- Kefka then resolved.
- Kefka was answered once, but the opponent immediately redeployed it and Squall could no longer contain the engine.

## Diagnosis
The early spells were all relevant against a blue interaction deck, but the opponent had an answer for each one while Squall stopped developing mana. Unlike Game 31's free-counter sequence, this game combined concentrated interaction with a genuine mana-development failure.

Notably, **Grand Abolisher** and **Voice of Victory** did not underperform on role; they were countered before they could establish the anti-interaction shield they are included to provide.

## Follow-up
Track Kefka/control games separately from Yuriko-style free-counter tempo. Continue monitoring whether the current land count solved general screw or whether isolated failures remain at an acceptable variance rate.
