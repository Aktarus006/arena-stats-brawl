# Game 50 — Kinnan

## Result
Loss.

## Key sequence
Squall successfully reached a position with **Squall, SeeD Mercenary** on the battlefield and **Deadly Rollick** available. The opponent was tapped out, creating what appeared to be a safe window to remove **Kinnan** when it was redeployed.

However, with the commander back on the battlefield the opponent had access to **Fierce Guardianship**, countering the interaction despite having no mana available. The opponent then followed the tempo swing with **Time Warp**, taking an extra turn and pulling decisively ahead.

## Diagnosis
This is a classic free-interaction swing rather than a simple lack-of-removal loss. Squall had both its commander and a premium free answer and chose a window where the opponent had no mana; **Fierce Guardianship** invalidated the normal mana constraint and Time Warp converted that exchange into a major tempo advantage.

The sequence reinforces the value of effects that create protected turns such as **Grand Abolisher**, **Voice of Victory**, **Ranger-Captain of Eos**, discard, or potentially other stack interaction. Merely increasing removal density cannot solve every free-counter sequence.
