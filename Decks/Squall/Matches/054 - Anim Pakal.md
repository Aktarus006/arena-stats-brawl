# Game 54 — Anim Pakal

## Result
Loss.

## Decisive sequence
**Lightning Greaves** created an anti-synergy with the evasion plan: once equipped to **Squall**, shroud prevented targeting Squall with **Key to the City** to make it unblockable.

Because Squall could not be made unblockable, the opponent was able to preserve enough of the board through combat. On the following turn, Squall was left without sufficient blockers and lost to the opposing pressure.

## Diagnosis
This is a useful sequencing / card-interaction lesson rather than evidence that either card is individually weak.

**Lightning Greaves** is excellent at protecting Squall from targeted interaction, but shroud is symmetrical: it also stops friendly targeted effects such as **Key to the City**. In matchups where connecting through blockers matters more than protecting from removal, equipping Greaves too early can reduce Squall's offensive options.

The presence of **Giver of Runes** and **Anim Pakal** also made combat increasingly difficult, reinforcing the importance of choosing between protection and evasion based on the opponent's board rather than automatically equipping Greaves.
