# Game 58 — Esika // The Prismatic Bridge

## Result
Win by early concession.

## Key sequence
The opponent was on the play, but **Gemstone Caverns** let Squall begin with an extra mana source by exiling **The Queen of Dale**.

- Turn 1: **Voice of Victory**.
- Turn 2: **Lightning Greaves** + **Shadowspear**.
- Turn 3: **Dark Ritual** enabled **Squall, SeeD Mercenary** while **Static Prison** was also available.
- Static Prison exiled the opponent's mana rock, disrupting development immediately before Squall could begin applying pressure.
- Opponent conceded.

## Diagnosis
A very strong disruptive-tempo opening. Gemstone Caverns effectively neutralized part of the disadvantage of being on the draw, Voice constrained interaction, the equipment package was pre-deployed, and Dark Ritual accelerated Squall while Static Prison simultaneously attacked the opponent's mana development.

This is a clean example of the deck's preferred sequencing: **early disruption/protection setup → accelerated Squall → remove the opponent's development piece → force them to answer from behind**.

The Queen of Dale was exiled to Gemstone Caverns, so this game provides no performance datapoint for Queen itself.
