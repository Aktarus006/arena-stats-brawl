# Squall — Card Performance

Ratings are based on **reported games**, not abstract card power. The 50-game audit is the baseline; this page emphasizes V2.1 observations.

## Strongly validated core

### Disruptor Flute
Repeatedly slows commander-centric decks and remains one of the most consistently useful disruption pieces.

### Drannith Magistrate
Repeatedly disrupts commander deployment and buys critical tempo.

### The Meathook Massacre
Validated anti-aggro stabilizer. G56 cleared five creatures after Vampiric Tutor found it.

### Lurrus of the Dream-Den
Important secondary engine. G52 worked with Plaguecrafter and Ral in a long win without casting Squall.

### Reanimate
High-ceiling recursion/theft. G55 converted Inquisition into a Trophy Mage theft line; G56 also used an opposing creature as a comeback resource.

### Recruiter of the Guard / Ranger-Captain of Eos
Core toolbox tutors. G59 especially showed Ranger-Captain becoming much stronger when Ral can return it for another search.

### Cloud, Midgar Mercenary
Strong equipment tutor. G57 found Buster Sword for a non-graveyard win plan.

### Skrelv, Defector Mite
G60 strongly validates Skrelv as protection plus recursion target: it protected Squall from red in response to Lightning Bolt, died, then returned through Squall's trigger to restore the protection engine.

### Sheoldred, the Apocalypse / Barrowgoyf / Ripples of Undeath / Dark Ritual
Remain strongly validated from the larger sample.

## V2.1 additions and recent interaction

### Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer
- G52: meaningful recursion/value in a long win without Squall.
- G59: major validation, returning Ranger-Captain for another tutor and later Accursed Marauder to answer reanimated Tymna.
- Current view: **validated secondary recursion/toolbox engine**.

### Sheltered by Ghosts
- G53: awkward in a creature-light Narset matchup.
- G57: lifelink was crucial in a long race after Valgavoth disabled graveyard recursion.
- Current view: **strongly positive; interaction + protection + lifelink gives it multiple useful modes**.

### Plaguecrafter
- G52: strong first showing alongside Lurrus/Ral.
- Current view: **positive; sacrifice interaction fills a real role**.

### Erode
- G56: removed opposing Tersa and enabled a later Reanimate line.
- Current view: **positive; broad interaction remains strategically important**.

### Sheoldred's Edict
- G53: cleanly answered Narset.
- G59: answered Tymna before Ral/Marauder handled her second appearance.
- Current view: **strongly positive against protected/must-answer commanders**.

### Get Lost
- G60: cleanly removed Sram before the opponent could establish the equipment/value engine.
- Current view: **positive; broad, efficient answer that supports tempo starts**.

## Context-dependent / watch

### Accursed Marauder
- G53 showed its floor against token-heavy/low-creature decks.
- G59 showed its ceiling when Ral returned it to remove reanimated Tymna.
- Current view: **keep; matchup-dependent but recursion raises the ceiling substantially**.

### Lightning Greaves
Historically strong, but G54 exposed sequencing anti-synergy with Key to the City due to shroud.

### Key to the City
Core evasion tool; especially useful in G57's alternate combat plan.

### Buster Sword
G57 provided its first clearly decisive positive datapoint after Cloud tutored it in a graveyard-locked game.

## V2.1 10-game checkpoint

Games 51–60 finished **6–4 (60.0%)**, compared with V2's 1–9. The most encouraging signal is not only the record but the diversity of successful cards/engines: Ral recursion, Sheltered lifelink, sacrifice effects, equipment lines, fast mana, Skrelv protection and Squall land recursion all contributed to wins.
