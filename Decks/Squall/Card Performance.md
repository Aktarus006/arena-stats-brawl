# Squall — Card Performance

Ratings are based on **reported games**, not abstract card power. The 50-game audit is the baseline; this page now emphasizes current V2.1 observations.

## Strongly validated core

### Disruptor Flute
Repeatedly slows commander-centric decks and remains one of the most consistently useful disruption pieces. G53: important enough that Vampiric Tutor was aimed at finding it vs Narset.

### Drannith Magistrate
Repeatedly disrupts commander deployment. G56 slowed Gut's development but was eventually removed. G57 kept Oura out of normal commander deployment for much of a long win.

### The Meathook Massacre
Validated anti-aggro stabilizer. G24 materially delayed Hawkeye; G56 Vampiric Tutor found Meathook X=1 and it cleared **five creatures**. The loss despite that reset reflects accumulated early pressure/mana denial, not poor Meathook performance.

### Lurrus of the Dream-Den
Important secondary engine. G52 worked with Plaguecrafter and Ral in a long Ketramose win achieved **without casting Squall**.

### Reanimate
High-ceiling recursion/theft. G55: Inquisition put opposing Trophy Mage in the graveyard and T3 Reanimate stole it, prompting concession. G56: attempted comeback by reanimating opposing Tersa after Erode removed it.

### Recruiter of the Guard
Core toolbox tutor; increasingly valuable as the creature interaction package expands.

### Ranger-Captain of Eos
G59 strongly validated the recursive tutor role: first found Mother of Runes, then after Toxic Deluge **Ral returned Ranger-Captain**, which found Esper Sentinel. Current view: **core recursive tutor/protected-turn piece**.

### Cloud, Midgar Mercenary
Strong equipment tutor. G57 found **Buster Sword**, enabling Squall's alternate combat plan after Valgavoth shut off graveyard recursion.

### Werefox Bodyguard / Skyclave Apparition / Kutzil's Flanker / Boggart Trawler
Remain core examples of the preferred design principle: independently useful interaction on creatures that can be tutored or recurred.

### Sheoldred, the Apocalypse / Barrowgoyf / Ripples of Undeath / Dark Ritual
Remain strongly validated from the first 50-game sample. Dark Ritual also powered the strong T3 Squall line in G58 and T4 Squall + Mother in G59.

## V2.1 additions and recent interaction

### Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer
- G52: meaningful recursion/value in a long win without Squall.
- G59: **major validation**. After Toxic Deluge, Ral returned Ranger-Captain for another tutor, then later returned Accursed Marauder to sacrifice a reanimated Tymna. Its +1 surveil also improved card quality while Sheoldred's Edict answered Tymna's first appearance.
- Current view: **strongly positive; one of V2.1's most important secondary engines**.

### Plaguecrafter
- G52: strong first showing alongside Lurrus/Ral while Ketramose was answered repeatedly.
- Current view: **positive; sacrifice interaction fills a real role**.

### Sheltered by Ghosts
- G53: awkward in a creature-light Narset matchup.
- G57: **major positive showing**. Its lifelink was crucial in a long race after Valgavoth disabled graveyard recursion, while Squall attacked with Buster Sword/Key support.
- Current view: **strongly trending positive; interaction + protection + lifelink gives it an important dual-purpose role**.

### Erode
- G56: removed opposing Tersa and created a later Reanimate line.
- Current view: **positive first meaningful observation; broad interaction remains strategically important**.

### Sheoldred's Edict
- G53: cleanly answered Narset.
- G59: answered Tymna's first deployment in a long win.
- Current view: **strongly positive; edict axis is important against protected/must-answer commanders**.

### Reprieve
- G49: useful interaction in a close game.
- Current view: **positive but small sample**.

## Context-dependent / watch

### Accursed Marauder
- G53 showed its floor when the opponent's only attacking bodies were Monk tokens and few non-token creatures were available.
- G59 showed its ceiling: T2 removed Thrasios, then Ral later reanimated Marauder to force sacrifice of a reanimated Tymna, prompting concession.
- Current view: **positive overall; matchup-dependent but excellent when the opposing plan centers on non-token commanders**.

### Lightning Greaves
- Historically strong protection/haste enabler.
- G54: shroud prevented Key to the City from targeting Squall, blocking the intended unblockable line and contributing to the loss.
- G58: part of a very strong pre-Squall setup before the opponent conceded.
- Current view: **still strong; sequencing caveat, not a cut signal**.

### Key to the City
- Core evasion tool.
- G54 reinforced that Key targeting must happen before Squall gains shroud from Greaves.
- G57 was again important as part of the alternate combat win with Buster Sword and Sheltered by Ghosts.

### Buster Sword
- Previously difficult to evaluate because it was not often decisive.
- G57: **important positive datapoint**. Cloud tutored it after Valgavoth shut off graveyard recursion, and it helped turn Squall into the primary combat plan.
- Current view: **positive watch; equipment toolbox redundancy may be more valuable than earlier results suggested**.

## V2.1 structural signal

G52, G57, G58 and G59 now show four different successful axes:
- **G52:** long game without casting Squall, through Plaguecrafter + Lurrus + Ral.
- **G57:** win despite graveyard recursion being shut off, via Drannith + Cloud/Buster Sword + Key + Sheltered lifelink.
- **G58:** explosive disruptive-tempo win with Gemstone Caverns, Voice, equipment, Dark Ritual/Squall and Static Prison.
- **G59:** grindy toolbox win through Force of Negation, Toxic Deluge, Dismember and Reanimate, with Ral repeatedly converting graveyard creatures into exact answers/value.

This diversity is the strongest evidence so far that the post-audit structure is improving.

## Life / mana watch

Life remains pressured by opposing aggro plus self-damage sources. G57 reinforces that **lifelink from Sheltered by Ghosts** can materially change long races. Mana problems must be classified carefully: G56 was primarily opponent-driven Strip Mine denial followed by no replacement land draws, rather than evidence by itself that the mana-base change failed.
