## Current experiments

### Squall — V2.1 (Games 51+)
- Current V2.1 record: **4–4 (50.0%)** after Game 58.
- V2.1 added **Loran of the Third Path, Sheltered by Ghosts, Plaguecrafter and Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer**.
- The experiment is testing a more interactive recursive-toolbox identity rather than commander-dependent recursion.
- Positive signals: **Plaguecrafter + Ral + Lurrus** won a long G52 without casting Squall; **Erode** was useful in G56; **Sheoldred's Edict** answered Narset in G53.
- G57 added resilience: **Valgavoth disabled graveyard recursion**, yet Drannith plus **Cloud → Buster Sword + Key + Sheltered by Ghosts lifelink** still won a long race.
- G58 showed the deck's explosive tempo ceiling while on the draw: **Gemstone Caverns → T1 Voice, T2 Greaves + Shadowspear, T3 Dark Ritual → Squall + Static Prison on a mana rock**, prompting concession.
- Fast aggro and noncreature engines remain important pressure points.
- Mana remains worth monitoring: G56 was heavily affected by opposing Strip Mine followed by failure to draw replacement lands.
